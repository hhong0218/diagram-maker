Org Chart Maker — an org chart tool that lays itself out

Org Chart Maker is a purpose-built tool for putting together a company org chart quickly. Start from the top box (your CEO or department head), then click "Add Report" to attach a team member underneath — the tree rearranges itself automatically every time. Use "Add Peer" to add a colleague who reports to the same manager. There's no dragging boxes into alignment or eyeballing spacing, which is what makes this faster than assembling a chart out of generic rectangles and lines.

Each box is a card with separate name and title fields: the name renders in bold, with the title underneath in smaller, lighter text, so every box stays visually consistent. Export your finished chart to PNG, SVG, or JSON, or copy a share link — everything runs in your browser with no sign-up and nothing installed.

Frequently asked questions

Does the org chart arrange itself automatically?
Yes. Select a box and click "Add Report" or "Add Peer" and the new box is placed automatically at the right level, with the connecting line drawn for you. There's no need to drag boxes into place by hand.
Why use a dedicated org chart card instead of a plain rectangle?
Each card has separate name and title fields, so every box stays consistently formatted. The name is shown in bold with the title in smaller, lighter text underneath, which reads better than a generic flowchart rectangle for this use case.
How do I save my finished org chart?
You can export to PNG, SVG, or JSON. PNG is great for pasting into slides or documents, SVG is ideal for high-resolution printing, and JSON is best for backups and re-editing. You can also copy a share link so others can view it.
Does it work on mobile?
Yes. As long as you have a browser, it works on both desktop and mobile. The bottom toolbar on phones and tablets lets you add a report and edit the selected box.
Do I need to sign up?
No. You can start working right away with no sign-up or login, and everything you create is stored only in your own browser and never sent to a server.
